Educator Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Educator Privacy

At TEACHER AI, we believe educational planning tools should respect and protect teachers. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our differentiated lesson-planning assistant.

2. What Data We Store

We believe in strict data minimization. We only store the minimal information necessary to deliver and operate the tool:

  • Google Account Email & Profile: When you sign in with Google, we store your Google email address and display name to authenticate your account and manage your monthly free generation quota (10 free plan generations per calendar month).
  • Lesson Plan History: The lesson plans, explanations, and exercises generated during your sessions are saved in your account history so that you can revisit, copy, and print your teaching materials at any time.
  • Session & Usage Timestamps: We log login timestamps and generation counts to prevent abuse and verify quota limits server-side.

Zero Data Selling Guarantee: Your data is NOT sold to anyone. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your email, user profile, or generated lesson plans with data brokers, advertisers, or any external third parties.

3. AI Processing & Content Generation

When you generate a lesson plan or ask follow-up questions, your topic and prompt are processed securely through our backend AI provider solely to generate your curriculum explanations and exercises. Your prompts are not used for advertising or marketing profiling.

4. Data Security

All authentication is handled via industry-standard Google Firebase Authentication. Communication between your browser and our servers is protected with TLS encryption. Counter limits and access logs are protected server-side with strict Firestore security rules.
